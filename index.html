A brand new fanmade sequel to Sonic Runners is COMING SOON!

Sonic Runners Deluxe aims to be a successor/sequel to the original Sonic Runners, with new power ups(wisps and shields), new characters and buddies, new stages, a revamped buddy system when paired with certain characters and much more!

The game will feature a brand-new story mode centered around the Phantom Ruby, along with multiple new events. These events will remain available even after they end, allowing you to unlock event items whenever you want.
